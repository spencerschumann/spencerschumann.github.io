Pulse probe

Finds out how to drive a Soundbrenner Pulse from a web page over Bluetooth, so Cello Coach can make it pulse without the Link bridge. It needs Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS or ChromeOS. Close the Soundbrenner app first, as the Pulse talks to one app at a time.

1. Connect

A web page can only use the Bluetooth services it names before connecting. The probe names some common ones and tries to read the services the Pulse advertises. If the Pulse's own service doesn't show up below, find its UUID with chrome://bluetooth-internals (desktop Chrome) or the nRF Connect app, paste it here and connect again.

Soundbrenner quick test

Uses the protocol Gadgetbridge worked out for the Soundbrenner Core: a 32-byte settings packet (tempo, time signature, accents, subdivision), then Start or Stop. The Pulse may use the same one. Connect first; the probe asks for the Core's services automatically.

4. Read what the Soundbrenner app sends

Android can record all Bluetooth traffic. Record the Soundbrenner app driving the Pulse, then open the recording here to see the bytes it writes.

  1. Settings → About phone: tap Build number seven times to turn on Developer options.
  2. Settings → System → Developer options: turn on Enable Bluetooth HCI snoop log (on some phones, choose "Enabled"), then turn Bluetooth off and on.
  3. In the Soundbrenner app, connect the Pulse and do one thing at a time, a few seconds apart, and note what you did: set the tempo to 60, start, wait 4 beats, stop; set 120, start, stop; change the beats per bar; change the vibration strength.
  4. Developer options → Take bug report (Interactive). When it is ready, share the .zip to your computer or open it on the phone.
  5. Choose the .zip (or a btsnoop_hci.log taken out of it) below. Turn the snoop log off again afterwards.

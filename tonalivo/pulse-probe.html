Pulse probe

Finds out how to drive a Soundbrenner Pulse from a web page over Bluetooth, so Tonalivo can make it pulse without the Link bridge. It needs Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS or ChromeOS. Close the Soundbrenner app first, as the Pulse talks to one app at a time.

1. Connect

A web page can only use the Bluetooth services it names before connecting. The probe names some common ones and tries to read the services the Pulse advertises. If the Pulse's own service doesn't show up below, find its UUID with chrome://bluetooth-internals (desktop Chrome) or the nRF Connect app, paste it here and connect again.

Find the Pulse’s own service

Web Bluetooth hides any service the page didn’t name in advance, and the Pulse’s isn’t published. Any one of these shows it:

  1. Desktop Chrome or Edge: open chrome://bluetooth-internals (or edge://bluetooth-internals) in a new tab, then Devices → Start scan → Inspect next to the Pulse. It lists every service; the long UUIDs that don’t start with 0000 are the Pulse’s own.
  2. Android: install nRF Connect (Nordic Semiconductor), scan, press Connect next to the Pulse, and note the services listed as “Unknown Service”. Long-press a UUID to copy it.
  3. This page: turn on chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features, restart Chrome, disconnect the Pulse, and choose it again: the probe then reads the services it advertises and adds them for you.

Paste the UUIDs into “More service UUIDs” above, press Choose device… again and pick the Pulse. Then send me the log (Copy or Download).

Soundbrenner Pulse test

The Pulse’s own protocol, worked out from a recording of the Soundbrenner app. The Pulse keeps its own beat; the probe sets the tempo, starts it, and keeps it in time the way the app does: it asks the Pulse where it is in the bar every few seconds and corrects it. Turn on “click along” to hear the beat the Pulse should match.

4. Read what the Soundbrenner app sends

Android can record all Bluetooth traffic. Record the Soundbrenner app driving the Pulse, then open the recording here to see the bytes it writes.

  1. Settings → About phone: tap Build number seven times to turn on Developer options.
  2. Settings → System → Developer options: turn on Enable Bluetooth HCI snoop log (on some phones, choose "Enabled"), then turn Bluetooth off and on.
  3. In the Soundbrenner app, connect the Pulse and do one thing at a time, a few seconds apart, and note what you did: set the tempo to 60, start, wait 4 beats, stop; set 120, start, stop; change the beats per bar; change the vibration strength.
  4. Developer options → Take bug report (Interactive). When it is ready, share the .zip to your computer or open it on the phone.
  5. Choose the .zip (or a btsnoop_hci.log taken out of it) below. Turn the snoop log off again afterwards.

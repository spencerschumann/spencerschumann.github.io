        My pieces

        Your own music in ABC notation, stored only in this browser. An .abc file can hold several tunes.

        ABC quick reference
        • K:D clef=bass key and clef (last header line); M:3/4 meter, L:1/8 unit length, Q:1/4=92 tempo.
        • Pitches: C = middle C, c an octave up; , lowers an octave: C,, is the open C string, G,, G, D, D, A, A.
        • ^F sharp, _B flat, =F natural (for the rest of the bar). The key signature applies otherwise.
        • Lengths in units of L: D2 twice as long, D/ half, D3/2 dotted; D>E dotted pair; (3DEF triplet; D2-D tie; z rest; [D,A,] double stop.
        • [M:2/4] at the start of a bar changes the meter from there on.
        • | bar line, |] end, |: … :| repeat, [1 … :|[2 endings. P:A on its own line starts a section.
        • !mf! dynamics, !crescendo(! !diminuendo(! hairpins, !1! or "^1" fingering, "G7" chord symbol for the piano.
        • A written piano part: more voices, e.g. V:pf clef=treble (the solo part is the voice named for an instrument, such as "Cello" or "Violin", else the first). %%accompaniment pulse (or waltz) changes how chord symbols are played; %%focus … adds a hint.

        Input

        Voice processing (echo cancellation, noise suppression, auto gain) is switched off so pitch and dynamics stay accurate. Place the device 1–2 m from your instrument.

        With Bluetooth headphones, choose the phone's or computer's built-in microphone here, not the headset's. A headset microphone puts the headphones into call mode, which cuts the sound quality both ways, and it points at your mouth rather than your instrument. On iPhone, iOS may switch to the headset microphone anyway; choose the iPhone microphone in Control Center if it does. Bluetooth also delays what you hear by roughly 150–250 ms: set the output latency once with the headphones connected (it is remembered for them), as the microphone can't hear the count-in clicks to measure it.

        Appearance

        Scoring

        If you're always a little behind the click, or the latency isn't calibrated, every note would otherwise show as late. With this on, each note is judged against your average timing and the average itself is reported separately.

        Piano accompaniment

        The grand piano (Splendid Grand Piano, via smplr) is downloaded the first time you use it and cached by the browser. If it can't be downloaded, a simpler synthesized piano is used.

        The app knows exactly what the piano plays, so it can tell the piano apart from your instrument. It works best when your instrument is louder than the piano at the microphone: put the device near you and the speaker a little further away, or use headphones.

        Tuning

        Latency

        Each device adds its own delay. The app remembers a value for every device by name, and switches when you change speakers, headphones, audio interface, microphone or screen.

        Delay from the app playing a click or a piano note to you hearing it. Starts at the browser's estimate; Bluetooth headphones often need 150–300 ms.

        Plays clicks through your speakers and listens for them. That measures output + microphone together (the round trip that grading uses), and sets the microphone's share. Don't use headphones for this.

        During the test, adjust until the flash and the click happen together. Monitors and TVs often lag more than a laptop's built-in screen.

        Plays clicks with the Pulse for 8 bars. Adjust the first slider until the vibration and the click happen together: higher if the Pulse comes after the click. With Link, the transport starts on the first bar line and stops after the last beat: if the Pulse misses the first beat or keeps going after the last click, raise the start/stop delay; if it starts before the first click or misses the last one, lower it. Needs the Pulse connected (Settings → Soundbrenner Pulse). Or let the app set it: lay the Pulse on the table next to the microphone and press "Listen for the Pulse" (Bluetooth only). With clicks (between the beats, through the speakers) the result doesn't depend on the microphone latency setting.

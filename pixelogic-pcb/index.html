Parts

A part is a whole circuit under a lid, its terminals named. Place one and wire its terminals like anything else; double-click it to look inside. To make one, build the circuit and select round it: Make part… does the rest. To change one, Decap it where it is (select it with Rearrange) — or Edit here for a loose copy — then rework it, select round it, and Make part… saves it back. A level’s part must still pass its level.
